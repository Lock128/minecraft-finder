# Privacy Policy for Gem, Ore & Struct Finder for MC

**Last Updated: November 18, 2026**

## Data Collection and Usage

### Summary
- The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising.
- World seeds, coordinates and search settings are never sent anywhere.
- Official builds send technical diagnostics (usage and performance data and error reports) to our processor Dash0 so we can keep the app reliable.
- We do not sell data.

### Diagnostics (Telemetry)
Official builds send technical diagnostics to our observability provider, Dash0, using the OpenTelemetry standard. The purpose is diagnostics and reliability: finding crashes, fixing errors and monitoring performance. We do not use it for advertising or tracking.

**What is collected:**
- Error and crash reports
- Page views (screen navigation) and usage events such as app lifecycle changes
- Performance data (startup and rendering timings, web vitals on the web)
- Device model and operating system version (native apps), or the browser user agent (web)
- App version
- A random session identifier, stored on your device. It is not tied to a person, and the app does not identify you or assign a user ID.

On iOS and Android, the app builds a user agent string that contains the app version, the operating system version and the device model identifier. The app version is also sent as the service version of the report.

**Network data:** The Dash0 server receives the IP address of each request. Dash0 anonymizes IP addresses and stores only an approximate location at city level.

**What is not collected:** account data, names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, your world seeds, coordinates or search inputs, and purchase data. Session replay and profiling are turned off.

**Processor and storage:** Dash0 processes the data on our behalf. Data is stored in the European Union (AWS eu-west-1, Ireland). Traces, logs, web events and profiles are kept for 30 days. Metrics are kept for 13 months.

**Not sold or shared for other purposes:** We do not sell data and do not share it with anyone other than Dash0 as our processor.

### Local Data Storage
Gem, Ore & Struct Finder for MC stores the following on your device:
- **Search Parameters**: Your last used world seed, coordinates, and search preferences are saved locally using Flutter's `shared_preferences` to improve user experience
- **App Settings**: Theme preferences and other app settings are stored locally
- **Diagnostics session identifier**: the random identifier described above

### Third-Party Services
The only third-party service that receives data from the app is Dash0 (diagnostics, described above). The app does not integrate with:
- Advertising networks
- Analytics services such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics
- Social media platforms
- Cloud storage services

### Data Sharing
We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. Search inputs stay on your device. Diagnostics are sent to Dash0 only as described above.

### Children's Privacy
This app is safe for children as it:
- Does not ask for or collect personal information such as names or email addresses
- Has no social features
- Contains no advertising
- Sends only the technical diagnostics described above
